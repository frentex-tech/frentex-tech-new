    <div class="eyebrow">
      "HOW WE WORK"
    <h1>
      "From the business question"
      <br>
      <span class="muted">
        "to a clear handover."
    <p>
      "A way of working that brings management, operations and technology together around one scope and deliverables you can review."
  <section class="process-detail wrap">
    <article class="enterprise-card">
      <span class="eyebrow">
        "01 / Discovery"
      <h2>
        "We understand the decision before the system."
      <p>
        "We meet with stakeholders to understand the workflow, the users, the constraints and the integrations."
      <strong>
        "Output:"
      <p>
        "A needs summary, a process map, and a list of requirements and priorities."
    <article class="enterprise-card">
      <span class="eyebrow">
        "02 / Scope and design"
      <h2>
        "What goes into the first release."
      <p>
        "We set the boundaries, design the user journeys and the prototype, and review dependencies, budget and schedule."
      <strong>
        "Output:"
      <p>
        "A technical and financial proposal, an interface prototype, acceptance criteria and approval to start."
    <article class="enterprise-card">
      <span class="eyebrow">
        "03 / Development and review"
      <h2>
        "Visible progress at every stage."
      <p>
        "We build in stages and review the product with the designated owner. We record decisions and changes, and test the scenarios."
      <strong>
        "Output:"
      <p>
        "Interim releases, a change log, tests and progress reports."
    <article class="enterprise-card">
      <span class="eyebrow">
        "04 / User acceptance and launch"
      <h2>
        "Go live with a transition plan."
      <p>
        "Your team tests the agreed business scenarios. We address the feedback and review migration, rollback and permissions."
      <strong>
        "Output:"
      <p>
        "An acceptance record, a launch plan, and an operations guide and training as scoped."
    <article class="enterprise-card">
      <span class="eyebrow">
        "05 / Handover and support"
      <h2>
        "Clear ownership and responsibilities."
      <p>
        "We hand over the repositories, documentation and accounts covered by the agreement, and define the follow-up after launch."
      <strong>
        "Output:"
      <p>
        "A handover record, support and maintenance details, and a plan for later improvements."
  <section class="section wrap">
    <div class="card-grid">
      <article class="enterprise-card">
        <h3>
          "Change management"
        <p>
          "Every out-of-scope request is documented and assessed for its impact on time and cost, then approved before it is added."
      <article class="enterprise-card">
        <h3>
          "Data handling"
        <p>
          "With your team, we define data classification, working environments, access permissions and hosting requirements. Sector requirements are part of discovery."
      <article class="enterprise-card">
        <h3>
          "Communication and governance"
        <p>
          "A point of contact on each side, agreed review meetings, and a log of decisions, risks and dependencies."
